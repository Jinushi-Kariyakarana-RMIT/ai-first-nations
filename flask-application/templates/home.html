Mangrove Detection - AI First Nations

Upload drone imagery to detect mangroves within the image

What is this tool?

This system uses machine learning to classify mangrove trees from drone imagery. Mangroves are critical coastal ecosystems that provide valuable carbon recapture. A feature that is becoming increasingly important for environmental monitoring and conservation.

Accurate Detection AI-trained models capable of classifying mangroves with a precision of over 80%
Designed for Conservation The system allows for monitoring of mangrove forest health, and can be scaled to automatically highlight changes over time.

How to use:

Select and upload an image (PNG, JPG, JPEG, or TIFF/TIF format) and click "Analyze" to begin processing. The system will return a classification indicating the species of mangrove detected..
