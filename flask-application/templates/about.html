About Mangrove AI

A practical AI First Nations tool for analysing drone and aerial imagery in support of mangrove monitoring and environmental work.

What It Does

Mangrove AI uses a staged machine-learning pipeline to detect mangrove vegetation and classify supported mangrove categories. A standalone tree-counting module is being developed to estimate visible trees across aerial imagery.

Analysis Capabilities

Mangrove Detection

Checks whether mangrove vegetation is visible.

Mangrove Classification

Reports one of the currently supported dataset categories.

Tree Counting

Estimates visible trees independently of classification.

Experimental

How It Works

  1. Upload
  2. Tile
  3. Detect
  4. Classify
  5. Count
  6. Results

Environmental Application

Consistent analysis of drone imagery can support vegetation monitoring, restoration assessment and future change-detection or carbon/GIS studies. Results remain model estimates and should be interpreted alongside field knowledge.
